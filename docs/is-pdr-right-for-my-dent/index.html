PDR suitability guide

Is paintless dent repair right for my dent?

Paintless dent repair works best when the original finish is intact and the metal can be carefully returned to shape. Photos help, but a hands-on inspection is the reliable way to confirm suitability.

Request an assessment

Signs your dent may qualify

Shallow, rounded dents and many ordinary door dings are common PDR candidates. Hail dents may also qualify when paint damage is absent.

  • Paint is intact without cracking or flaking
  • Metal is not severely stretched
  • A technician can reach or safely pull the damaged area
  • Damage is on a metal body panel rather than broken plastic

When conventional body repair may be better

PDR is not automatically the right repair for every dent. A provider should be willing to recommend conventional work when it will produce a safer or more complete result.

  • Paint is cracked, deeply scratched, or missing
  • Panel metal is sharply folded or badly stretched
  • The panel has previous filler or a poor repaint
  • Structural components or safety systems may be involved

How to photograph the damage

Clean the panel, avoid harsh direct sun, and photograph the dent from several angles. Reflections from a garage opening, parking-lot lines, or a striped object can make the panel distortion easier to see. Include one wider photo showing the panel and one close photo showing the dent.
