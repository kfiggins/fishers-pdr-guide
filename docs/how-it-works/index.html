How our Fishers provider-matching service works

Fishers Dent Repair Guide is an independent information and referral website. We are not a body shop and do not represent ourselves as the technician who will repair your vehicle.

Request an assessment

1. You send a request

Tell us about the vehicle and damage. Photos can make the first review more useful, but no online assessment is a final repair diagnosis or quote.

2. We review and route it

We check whether the request appears relevant and may share it with one independent provider serving Fishers. Providers are responsible for their own licensing, insurance, workmanship, prices, scheduling, and statements.

3. You decide what happens next

A provider may contact you to ask questions or arrange an inspection. You are not required to hire anyone, and any service agreement is between you and that provider.

How this website may be paid

A participating provider may pay us for advertising, referrals, or qualified leads. That arrangement does not increase your obligation and does not guarantee that a provider is the lowest-priced or only available option.
