Paintless dent repair cost in Fishers

There is no responsible flat price for a dent that has not been seen. Size matters, but location, access, depth, metal stretch, and total dent count can matter just as much.

Request an assessment

What changes the price

A small open-panel dent is usually simpler than a dent on a reinforced body line or beneath trim that must be removed.

  • Dent diameter, depth, and sharpness
  • Panel location and backside access
  • Aluminum versus steel panels
  • Body-line or edge involvement
  • Number and concentration of hail dents
  • Required removal and reinstallation of trim or interior panels

Single dents versus hail damage

Single dents are commonly estimated individually. Hail damage is generally mapped across each affected panel, and an insurer may be involved when a comprehensive claim is opened. Your deductible and policy terms determine insurance coverage—not this website or a repair provider.

How to compare estimates

Ask whether the estimate includes trim removal, taxes, and any expected supplemental work. Confirm who will perform the repair, whether the result is guaranteed, and what happens if PDR cannot safely achieve the expected finish.
